Research in focus

Five connected research streams

01 Modelling & experiment

Ferroelectric MEMS

I connect nonlinear material modelling with device-scale simulation and experimental characterization of ferroelectric thin-film MEMS. Inverse identification links measured electromechanical response to material parameters.

02 Fabrication & characterization

Flexoelectric MEMS & Microfabrication

Higher-order electromechanical models meet thin-film fabrication and experiment. This modelling–fabrication–characterization loop helps extract weak flexoelectric responses from silicon-nitride specimens.

Selected work

  1. Observation of flexoelectric effect in PECVD silicon nitride2025 ↗
  2. NURBS-based formulation for nonlinear electro-gradient elasticity in semiconductors2019 ↗

03 Learning & differentiable simulation

Scientific Machine Learning & Differentiable Mechanics

I use physics-informed learning and neural operators for mechanics problems shaped by sparse measurements and history dependence. Differentiable finite elements connect forward simulation, parameter sensitivities, and inverse identification.

Selected work

  1. Differentiable finite elements for history-dependent ferroelectricity: matrix-free forward analysis and adjoint inverse identification2026 ↗
  2. Physics-informed neural networks with data-driven in modeling and characterizing piezoelectric micro-bender2024 ↗
  3. Fourier neural operator-based modeling of ferroelectric device under large signal and high frequency2026 ↗
Explore this research →

04 Microstructure & effective behaviour

Multiscale Computational Mechanics

FFT-based homogenization resolves heterogeneous microstructures and their internal fields. Deep material and kinematic networks provide reduced representations for history-dependent, differentiable multiscale modelling.

Selected work

  1. Universal Fourier Neural Operators for periodic homogenization problems in linear elasticity2026 ↗
  2. Symmetries in stochastic homogenization and adjustments for the RVE method2026 ↗
Explore this research →

05 Nonlinearity & wave propagation

Nonlinear & Topological Phononics

Nonlinear mechanics makes wave-guiding structures reconfigurable. Soft and bistable unit cells connect local deformation and structural switching with distinct topological states.

Selected work

  1. Tunable topological bandgaps and frequencies in a pre-stressed soft phononic crystal2019 ↗
  2. Topologically switchable behavior induced by an elastic instability in a phononic waveguide2020 ↗
