05 / Nonlinearity & wave propagation

Nonlinear & Topological Phononics

Nonlinear mechanics makes wave-guiding structures reconfigurable. Soft and bistable unit cells connect local deformation and structural switching with distinct topological states.

OverviewSelected work 2

My work in topological phononics investigates how nonlinear mechanics can make wave-guiding structures reconfigurable. I showed that large deformation of soft unit cells described by hyperelastic material models can break inversion symmetry and drive transitions between distinct topological phases.

I subsequently introduced a bistable beam unit cell whose two stable configurations possess different topological invariants. The mechanical state can therefore switch the topological response without continuously maintaining an external stimulus. This work links local structural reconfiguration, nonlinear mechanics, and collective wave propagation, and remains a distinctive part of my research on programmable mechanical systems.
