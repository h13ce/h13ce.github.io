My multiscale work focuses on connecting resolved microstructural mechanics with efficient representations of effective material behaviour. I use FFT-based computational homogenization to resolve heterogeneous microstructures and their internal fields, and deep material networks to compress this response into trainable hierarchical constitutive representations.

The present programme extends these ideas to history-dependent and differentiable multiscale modelling. This includes adjoint formulations for training deep material networks with inelastic constituents and deep kinematic networks that encode mechanics-informed reductions. The broader objective is to retain the essential microstructural physics while making multiscale models efficient enough for inverse analysis and integration into larger-scale simulations.
