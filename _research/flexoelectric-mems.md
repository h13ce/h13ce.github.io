Flexoelectricity couples electric polarization to strain gradients and therefore introduces higher-order kinematics into electromechanical modelling. I developed isogeometric formulations that provide the spatial continuity required by these models and extended them to coupled problems involving interfaces, free charge, and semiconducting materials.

The computational work was subsequently connected to experiment through my MSCA project on thin-film flexoelectricity. The project combined microfabrication of approximately 200 nm silicon-nitride films, electrical measurement, controlled mechanical loading, and finite-element interpretation to extract the flexoelectric response of the material. This modelling–fabrication–characterization loop is representative of how I approach weak multiphysics effects that cannot be identified reliably from simulation or measurement alone.
